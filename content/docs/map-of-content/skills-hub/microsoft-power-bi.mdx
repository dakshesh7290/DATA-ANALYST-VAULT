> A business intelligence platform for building interactive, live dashboards and reports on top of a proper data model.

## What It Is

Microsoft's BI tool, made up of Power BI Desktop (for building reports) and Power BI Service (for publishing and sharing them), built around data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visuals.

## What It Does

Connects to one or more data sources, models the relationships between them, calculates metrics using DAX, and presents everything through interactive, filterable dashboards that can refresh automatically.

## When It's Used

- Replacing static, manually updated Excel reports with live, self-service dashboards
- Reporting scenarios requiring multiple related data sources modeled together
- Giving stakeholders the ability to explore and filter data themselves
- Sharing standardized, centrally maintained reports across a team or organization
