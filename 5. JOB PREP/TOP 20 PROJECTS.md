# Top 20 Projects

> Twenty practical Data Analyst project ideas spanning different industries, each with enough detail to scope and execute a genuine end-to-end analysis.

## Overview

These project ideas are designed to build a varied, portfolio-ready body of work — covering different industries, different core skills, and different types of business questions. Pick a handful (see [[PORTFOLIO BUILDING GUIDE]] for guidance on scope) rather than attempting all twenty shallowly.

---

### 1. E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis
- **Industry:** E-commerce
- **Business problem:** Which products, categories, and channels drive the most revenue and profit?
- **Dataset needs:** Order-level transaction data with product, category, price, quantity, channel, and date fields
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Excel or Power BI
- **KPIs:** Revenue, units sold, average order value, profit margin
- **Analysis questions:** Which categories are growing/declining? Which channels have the best margins?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Revenue by category/channel over time, top products table
- **Expected insights:** Identify underperforming categories or overreliance on a single channel
- **Difficulty:** Beginner · **Resume/Portfolio value:** High for demonstrating core reporting skills

### 2. Customer Churn Analysis (Subscription Business)
- **Industry:** SaaS / Subscription
- **Business problem:** Which customer segments are most likely to churn, and why?
- **Dataset needs:** Customer records with subscription start/end dates, usage metrics, plan type
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Python (Pandas), Statistics
- **KPIs:** Churn rate, retention rate, customer lifetime value
- **Analysis questions:** Does usage frequency predict churn? Do certain plans churn more than others?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Churn rate by segment/plan, cohort retention curves
- **Expected insights:** A specific at-risk segment defined by usage or plan characteristics
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** High — churn analysis is a very common real-world request

### 3. Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis
- **Industry:** Marketing
- **Business problem:** Which marketing campaigns delivered the best return on investment?
- **Dataset needs:** Campaign spend, impressions, clicks, conversions by channel and time period
- **Skills/Tools:** Excel, SQL, Power BI
- **KPIs:** Cost per acquisition, conversion rate, ROI
- **Analysis questions:** Which channels are most cost-effective? Are there diminishing returns at higher spend?
- **Dashboard ideas:** ROI by channel, spend vs conversions scatter plot
- **Expected insights:** Reallocation recommendation across channels based on efficiency
- **Difficulty:** Beginner · **Resume/Portfolio value:** High for marketing-adjacent roles

### 4. HR Attrition Analysis
- **Industry:** Human Resources
- **Business problem:** What factors are associated with employee turnover?
- **Dataset needs:** Employee records with tenure, department, performance, compensation, exit status
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Python, Statistics
- **KPIs:** Attrition rate by department/tenure band
- **Analysis questions:** Are certain departments or tenure ranges at higher risk? Is attrition linked to compensation bands?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Attrition rate by department, tenure distribution of leavers
- **Expected insights:** A specific department or tenure range with elevated attrition risk
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Good for demonstrating cross-domain versatility

### 5. Financial Statement / Budget Variance Analysis
- **Industry:** Finance
- **Business problem:** Where is actual spend diverging most from budget, and why?
- **Dataset needs:** Budgeted vs actual figures by department/category and period
- **Skills/Tools:** Excel, SQL
- **KPIs:** Budget variance (absolute and percentage), by department
- **Analysis questions:** Which departments consistently over/underspend? Are variances trending or one-off?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Variance waterfall chart, trend of variance by department
- **Expected insights:** Departments with structurally miscalibrated budgets vs one-time anomalies
- **Difficulty:** Beginner · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Strong for finance-focused analyst roles

### 6. Retail Inventory & Stockout Analysis
- **Industry:** Retail
- **Business problem:** Where are stockouts costing the business the most in lost sales?
- **Dataset needs:** Inventory levels, sales, and stockout events by store/product over time
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Excel, Power BI
- **KPIs:** Stockout rate, estimated lost sales, inventory turnover
- **Analysis questions:** Which products/stores have the highest stockout frequency? What's the estimated revenue impact?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Stockout heatmap by store/product, inventory turnover ranking
- **Expected insights:** A specific product category or store cluster driving most stockout-related loss
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Good for retail/operations-focused roles

### 7. Customer Segmentation Analysis
- **Industry:** Customer Analytics (cross-industry)
- **Business problem:** What natural customer segments exist based on behavior and value?
- **Dataset needs:** Customer transaction history (recency, frequency, monetary value)
- **Skills/Tools:** Python (Pandas), Statistics
- **KPIs:** RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) scores per segment
- **Analysis questions:** What distinct segments emerge, and how should each be treated differently?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Segment size and value comparison, RFM distribution
- **Expected insights:** A small number of clearly defined, actionable segments
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** High — demonstrates applied statistical thinking

### 8. Website/Product Funnel Analysis
- **Industry:** Product / SaaS
- **Business problem:** Where are users dropping off in the signup or purchase funnel?
- **Dataset needs:** Event-level user data across funnel stages (view, signup, activation, purchase)
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL (window functions), Python
- **KPIs:** Conversion rate at each funnel stage
- **Analysis questions:** Which stage has the steepest drop-off? Does drop-off vary by acquisition channel?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Funnel visualization, drop-off rate by channel
- **Expected insights:** A specific funnel stage as the priority for improvement
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** High for product analyst-track roles

### 9. A/B Test Analysis (Simulated or Public Dataset)
- **Industry:** Product / Marketing
- **Business problem:** Did a tested change (e.g. new page design) produce a statistically significant improvement?
- **Dataset needs:** Control/treatment group data with a defined success metric
- **Skills/Tools:** Statistics (hypothesis testing), Python or SQL
- **KPIs:** Conversion rate difference, p-value, confidence interval
- **Analysis questions:** Is the observed difference statistically and practically significant?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Control vs treatment comparison, confidence interval visualization
- **Expected insights:** A clear, statistically grounded recommendation (adopt, reject, or extend the test)
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate–Advanced · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Very high — directly demonstrates statistical rigor

### 10. Supply Chain / Logistics Delay Analysis
- **Industry:** Supply Chain
- **Business problem:** What factors are associated with shipment delays?
- **Dataset needs:** Shipment records with origin, destination, carrier, promised vs actual delivery dates
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Python
- **KPIs:** On-time delivery rate, average delay by carrier/route
- **Analysis questions:** Which carriers or routes underperform consistently?
- **Dashboard ideas:** On-time rate by carrier, delay distribution by route
- **Expected insights:** A specific carrier or route segment driving most delays
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Good for operations-focused roles

### 11. SaaS Product Usage & Feature Adoption Analysis

- **Business problem:** Which features are driving engagement, and which are underused?
- **Dataset needs:** Feature-level usage events per user over time
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Python, Power BI
- **KPIs:** Feature adoption rate, active usage frequency
- **Analysis questions:** Does adoption of a specific feature correlate with retention?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Feature adoption funnel, usage frequency by feature
- **Expected insights:** A feature strongly associated with retention worth promoting further

### 12. Healthcare Appointment No-Show Analysis (Conceptual/Public Data)

- **Dataset needs:** Appointment records with scheduling lead time, patient demographics (anonymized/public), no-show flag

- **Analysis questions:** Does longer scheduling lead time predict higher no-show rates?
- **Dashboard ideas:** No-show rate by lead time bucket, day-of-week pattern
- **Expected insights:** A specific scheduling factor strongly associated with no-shows

### 19. Subscription Revenue (MRR/ARR) Trend Analysis

- **Dataset needs:** Subscription-level data with plan changes, upgrades, downgrades, cancellations by date

- **Dataset needs:** Multi-touch customer interaction data across channels leading to conversion
- **Skills/Tools:** SQL, Python
- **KPIs:** Channel contribution to conversion (multi-touch attribution)
- **Analysis questions:** Which channel combinations most reliably lead to conversion?
- **Dashboard ideas:** Channel path visualization, attribution comparison across models
- **Expected insights:** A clearer picture of which channels are under- or over-credited by simple attribution
- **Difficulty:** Advanced · **Resume/Portfolio value:** Very high — demonstrates sophisticated marketing analytics thinking

## How to Choose Projects for Your Portfolio

Aim for variety across:
- **Industries** — don't pick five projects from the same domain
- **Skills** — ensure your chosen set collectively demonstrates SQL, Python, statistics, and visualization, not just one tool
- **Difficulty** — a mix of approachable and more advanced projects shows range
- **Business function** — sales, marketing, operations, finance, product — pick a spread rather than all-sales-dashboards
